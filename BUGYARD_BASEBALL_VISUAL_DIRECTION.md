# Bugyard Baseball — visual direction
## Target
Late-1990s hand-illustrated computer sports adventure: summer afternoon, exaggerated cartoon personalities, warm sandy base paths, lush textured lawn, wooden fences, low neighborhood houses, oversized caps and expressive poses. Original Dan universe artwork, thick dark-brown outlines, cream/orange app shell and forest-green scoreboard. No borrowed Backyard Baseball sprites or exact screen layouts.
## Composition
Character draft resembles a neighborhood team photo: portrait cards, four readable ratings, selected-team indication. At bat, camera looks from behind home plate toward a small pitcher and distant outfield, with oversized foreground hitter, wooden bat, white chalk lines, plate and strike zone. Pitching reverses emphasis: pitcher in foreground, target at plate. Contact cuts to overhead diamond, with ball trajectory and runners. Scoreboard remains visible. Tap controls grouped below illustration; clear count and context-specific prompt.
## Fields
Crunch County Sandlot: wooden fence, garden, trees, sky, shed and chip advertisement.
Abuela's Courtyard: terracotta rooflines, palms, warm ochre dirt, Spanish courtyard.
Ethnuffy's Lunar League: purple grass, cratered horizon, ringed planet, pale chalk.
## Character design
Dan: original red bug, equipped cosmetic retained. Frog/Toad: bright green and earthy brown silhouettes. Ethnuffy: purple cosmic creature. Baby: giant gray cat with cream belly. Matteo and supporting original bug cast have distinguishable colors and caps. All characters have four ratings, short personality quips, and team accent caps/jerseys. Fielders remain small and recognizable.
## Animation
Pitch travels toward plate with depth scaling; swing pivots bat and hitter; hit view draws ball flight, fielder chase and runner movement. Fielding uses a moving timing cursor. Home run celebration, inning change and end screen. Avoid full-screen flashing; honor reduced motion. Art must be original SVG/CSS rendered inside game, not just a concept mockup.
